Analyst Brief

The 10-year Treasury sits at 4.263% and the dollar index at 103.81, creating a fixed-income structure that reflects a market paying for geopolitical risk. Yields are rising while the dollar remains strong, which typically tightens financial conditions and pressures emerging markets. This setup implies a stagflationary environment where the Fed may have to maintain a hawkish stance despite the war. The real yield is still low, suggesting the bond market is pricing in a higher term premium, which could eventually spill over into equities.

The correlation between bonds and equities has become more negative, but not in a typical way. Normally, when stocks fall, bonds rally, but the war is driving both yields and risk premiums up, breaking the diversification benefit. Bond behavior is now gating equity allocations: if yields continue to rise, the equity risk premium will compress, forcing investors to reduce their risky positions. The strong dollar also hurts multinational earnings, further complicating the equity landscape. This cross-asset dynamic is a key signal for portfolio positioning.

The S&P 500 is at 5,212, but the technical structure is showing severe divergences. The index is making new highs while the RSI is making lower highs, a bearish divergence. Meanwhile, Bitcoin has broken out, moving 3.94% higher to $80,490, while the S&P remains stagnant. This divergence suggests a potential rotation out of equities into crypto, or a leading indicator of a broader risk-off move. The breakdown between the two asset classes is unusual and highlights the fragility of the equity rally.

My directional bias is bearish for the short term. The combination of a war, low volatility, and a complacent market creates an asymmetric risk to the downside. For validation, I am watching the S&P 500 level of 5,200; a break below could trigger a sell-off. If the 10-year yield moves above 4.5%, that would also confirm the bearish thesis. I expect a pullback to the 5,100-5,200 range, with a hard stop at 5,300. The risk-reward is clearly skewed to the downside.
