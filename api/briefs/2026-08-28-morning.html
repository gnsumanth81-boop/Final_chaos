Analyst Brief

This backdrop creates a headwind for equities and crypto, as liquidity tightens. Equities and crypto have shown high correlation recently, but bond yields are the gating factor. As yields rise, the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets like BTC increases. The S&P 500 at 5,253 is being propped up by AI optimism, but that narrative is fragile. If yields push higher, we could see a simultaneous sell-off in both stocks and crypto, as the risk premium adjusts.

The VIX at 15.24 is complacent, but the put/call ratio and breadth indicators are deteriorating. The market is ignoring geopolitical risks, which could trigger a sharp reversal.

Our bias is bearish for the near term. We expect a correction in risk assets, driven by a combination of high valuations, tightening financial conditions, and geopolitical shocks. We are shorting BTC and buying puts on COIN, while positioning in defensive names like BRK.B. The 2% solution refers to the potential for a 2% rate cut, which would be a catalyst for a bounce, but we don't see that coming soon.
